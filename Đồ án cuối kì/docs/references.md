# Tài liệu tham khảo đã đối chiếu

Chuẩn trích dẫn thống nhất: IEEE. Số trong ngoặc vuông trong báo cáo và ghi chú
nghiên cứu tham chiếu các mục dưới đây. DOI dẫn tới metadata/landing page; thông
tin dataset được đối chiếu với `configs/uci_501.json` và UCI Repository.

1. S. X. Chen, “Beijing Multi-Site Air-Quality Data,” UCI Machine Learning
   Repository, 2017, DOI: [10.24432/C5RK5G](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5RK5G).
   Dataset 501, giấy phép CC BY 4.0. Metadata dự án ghi nhận 12 trạm, 420,768
   dòng và 18 cột.
2. A. E. Hoerl and R. W. Kennard, “Ridge Regression: Biased Estimation for
   Nonorthogonal Problems,” *Technometrics*, vol. 12, no. 1, pp. 55–67, 1970,
   DOI: [10.1080/00401706.1970.10488634](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.1970.10488634).
3. R. Tibshirani, “Regression Shrinkage and Selection Via the Lasso,” *Journal
   of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B (Methodological)*, vol. 58, no. 1,
   pp. 267–288, 1996, DOI:
   [10.1111/j.2517-6161.1996.tb02080.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1996.tb02080.x).
4. H. Zou and T. Hastie, “Regularization and Variable Selection Via the Elastic
   Net,” *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B (Statistical
   Methodology)*, vol. 67, no. 2, pp. 301–320, 2005, DOI:
   [10.1111/j.1467-9868.2005.00503.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2005.00503.x).
5. L. Breiman, “Random Forests,” *Machine Learning*, vol. 45, pp. 5–32, 2001,
   DOI: [10.1023/A:1010933404324](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1010933404324).
6. J. H. Friedman, “Greedy Function Approximation: A Gradient Boosting
   Machine,” *The Annals of Statistics*, vol. 29, no. 5, pp. 1189–1232, 2001,
   DOI: [10.1214/aos/1013203451](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1013203451).
7. D. H. Wolpert, “Stacked Generalization,” *Neural Networks*, vol. 5, no. 2,
   pp. 241–259, 1992, DOI:
   [10.1016/S0893-6080(05)80023-1](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0893-6080(05)80023-1).
8. C. Bergmeir and J. M. Benítez, “On the Use of Cross-Validation for Time
   Series Predictor Evaluation,” *Information Sciences*, vol. 191, pp. 192–213,
   2012, DOI: [10.1016/j.ins.2011.12.028](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ins.2011.12.028).
9. F. Pedregosa et al., “Scikit-learn: Machine Learning in Python,” *Journal
   of Machine Learning Research*, vol. 12, pp. 2825–2830, 2011. URL:
   [https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v12/pedregosa11a.html](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v12/pedregosa11a.html).
