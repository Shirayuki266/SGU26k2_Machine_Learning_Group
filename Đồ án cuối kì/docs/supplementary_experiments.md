`2512bc9c844a4b39a150c52f6bded9db`. It evaluated the predeclared 13 estimator
configurations and two causal baselines using three expanding train-only folds.

## Experiment-by-experiment record

All validation values below use the same 102,479 primary IDs. They are
development results, not test scores. The reference is the unchanged locked RF
A recipe (`n_estimators=80`, `max_depth=12`, `min_samples_leaf=2`), with
validation MAE 9.5615 and RMSE 18.9648.

### 1. Three-hour rolling-mean baseline

- **Hypothesis:** The mean of the three most recent observed PM2.5 values may
  improve on persistence without fitting a model.
- **Configuration:** Use T07's complete trailing three-hour window; fall back
  to PM2.5(t) during warmup.
- **Results:** Validation MAE/RMSE were 14.0165/26.8095.
- **Comment and decision:** It was substantially worse than locked RF A. Keep
  it as a causal baseline comparison.

### 2. Same-target-hour-yesterday baseline

- **Hypothesis:** Daily periodicity may make PM2.5 at the previous day's target
  hour a stronger no-fit baseline.
- **Configuration:** Read the same station's observation at target time minus
  24 hours only if it is available by origin t; otherwise fall back to
  PM2.5(t).
- **Results:** Validation MAE/RMSE were 50.4168/84.3504.
- **Comment and decision:** It was substantially worse than locked RF A. Keep
  it as a causal baseline comparison; no model is fit.

### 3. RF B with locked hyperparameters

- **Hypothesis:** Adding causal lag/rolling features may improve RF at the
  unchanged locked hyperparameters.
- **Configuration:** Compare feature set A and B using the locked RF recipe:
  80 trees, depth 12, and minimum leaf size 2.
- **Results:** RF B reached validation MAE/RMSE 9.3798/18.6447, versus
  9.5615/18.9648 for RF A; B's MAE was 0.1817 (1.90%) lower.
- **Comment and decision:** This is a supplementary candidate only. The
  T12/T13 frozen recipe remains RF A.

### 4. Bounded RF B tuning

- **Hypothesis:** A finite search over tree count, depth, and leaf size may
  improve on locked-parameter RF B.
- **Configuration:** Compare only the predeclared `40 trees/depth 8/leaf 5` and
  `80 trees/depth 8/leaf 5` variants; select using pooled train-CV MAE.
- **Results:** Pooled CV MAE was 9.8955 for the smaller variant and 9.8872 for
  `rf_B_tuned_more_trees`; only the latter was evaluated on validation
  (MAE/RMSE 9.5093/18.7612). The smaller candidate was not refit on validation.
- **Comment and decision:** Neither tuning variant beat locked RF B on CV.
  Keep the logged outcomes; do not change the frozen recipe.

### 5. HistGradientBoosting

- **Hypothesis:** Squared versus absolute loss and A versus B inputs may yield a
  useful alternative to RF.
- **Configuration:** Four predeclared combinations (`A/B ×
  squared_error/absolute_error`), with `learning_rate=0.1`, `max_iter=100`,
  `max_leaf_nodes=15`, and `l2_regularization=1`; choose by pooled train-CV MAE.
- **Results:** `hgb_B_absolute` was the family winner and reached validation
  MAE/RMSE 9.4868/20.1625. Its MAE was 0.0747 (0.78%) below locked RF A.
- **Comment and decision:** This small validation advantage is descriptive
  only. Keep the candidate result; do not replace the T12/T13 locked RF A.

### 6. Lasso and ElasticNet on feature set B

- **Hypothesis:** Sparse linear regularization on B may be competitive and may
  retain a different set of source feature groups than T12's selected C set.
- **Configuration:** Scale within the train-fitted T08 pipeline; compare Lasso
  `alpha=0.1` and `1.0`, and ElasticNet `alpha=0.1`, `l1_ratio=0.5`, each
  using the declared three expanding train folds. Select by pooled train-CV
  MAE.
- **Results:** Lasso `alpha=0.1` won this family on CV (pooled MAE 10.1203) and
  scored validation MAE/RMSE 9.8143/18.4641. Its validation MAE was 0.2528
  (2.64%) above locked RF A.
- **Feature-group comparison:** The exact Lasso validation recipe was refit
  only on the 204,865 T05 train rows to inspect nonzero coefficients; predictions
  reproduce the saved Lasso validation artifact with maximum absolute
  difference `2.3e-13`. No run was added to the ledger. A source group is
  retained when at least one of its encoded coefficients is nonzero; one-hot
  columns and missing indicators are grouped by their original T07 feature.
  Lasso retained 23 of the 33 B groups:
  `PM2.5`, `PM10`, `SO2`, `NO2`, `CO`, `TEMP`, `DEWP`, `RAIN`, `WSPM`,
  `target_hour`, `target_month`, `target_day_of_week`, `target_hour_sin`,
  `target_hour_cos`, `target_month_sin`, `target_month_cos`, `pm25_lag_1h`,
  `pm25_lag_12h`, `pm25_lag_24h`, `pm25_roll_std_3h`,
  `pm25_roll_mean_24h`, `station`, and `wd`.

  T12's final 12-group C selection (validation run
  `3a9f2500e4dd4ef28d7bc20def6f79c8`) shares nine groups with Lasso:
  `PM2.5`, `PM10`, `TEMP`, `DEWP`, `RAIN`, `WSPM`, `target_hour_sin`,
  `target_month_cos`, and `pm25_lag_1h`. C-only groups are `PRES`,
  `pm25_roll_mean_3h`, and `pm25_roll_mean_6h`; Lasso-only groups are `SO2`,
  `NO2`, `CO`, `target_hour`, `target_month`, `target_day_of_week`,
  `target_hour_cos`, `target_month_sin`, `pm25_lag_12h`, `pm25_lag_24h`,
  `pm25_roll_std_3h`, `pm25_roll_mean_24h`, `station`, and `wd`.
- **Comment and decision:** These are coefficient-based associations, not
  causal or independent feature-importance claims. Lasso did not improve
  validation MAE over locked RF A; the comparison is descriptive and does not
  alter T12's C recipe.

### 7. Decision Tree

- **Hypothesis:** A shallow single tree may provide a simpler error/complexity
  reference for the ensemble models.
- **Configuration:** B features with `max_depth` 6 or 12 and
  `min_samples_leaf=50`; choose the depth by pooled train-CV MAE.
- **Results:** The depth-12 winner reached validation MAE/RMSE 9.8648/19.1801.
- **Comment and decision:** It was worse than locked RF A on validation. Retain
  it as a simple-model comparison, not as a deployment candidate.

### 8. Fusion-3

- **Hypothesis:** Averaging complementary model-family predictions selected
  using train OOF may improve validation error.
- **Configuration:** Select three distinct-family champions by pooled train-CV
  MAE, then average their aligned fold OOF predictions and validation
  predictions; validation labels do not select members.
- **Results:** Validation MAE/RMSE were 9.2746/18.5055; MAE was 0.2869 (3.00%)
  below locked RF A.
- **Comment and decision:** This is the best validation result for the
  predeclared mean ensemble, but it does not change the locked model.

### 9. OOF Ridge Stack

- **Hypothesis:** A Ridge meta-learner on aligned base-model OOF predictions may
  combine their strengths.
- **Configuration:** `Ridge(alpha=1)` fit only on the same three expanding-fold
  train OOF predictions; validation predictions are scored only after meta-fit.
- **Results:** Validation MAE/RMSE were 9.2321/18.4718, the lowest MAE in this
  supplementary comparison. MAE was 0.3294 (3.45%) below locked RF A.
- **Comment and decision:** Keep as an evaluation candidate only. Validation was
  one split, many candidates were compared, and no statistical test was
  performed; this is not evidence of a reliable generalization advantage.

### Overall validation interpretation

Stack had the lowest validation MAE; Fusion-3, locked-parameter RF B, and
HistGradientBoosting B/absolute also had lower validation MAE than locked RF A.
The differences are descriptive for one shared split only: there are no
confidence intervals or significance tests, and repeated model comparison can
favor a candidate by chance. No T17b candidate was scored on test data, and
RF A remains the frozen T12/T13 model.

## Execution and audit notes

The original T13 run archive was restored with the official script after
verifying SHA-256
`5362ebf0c51961d96537f5c3a8ffae39b4e1da0b7f6daa6230593445a8e471c1`.
Restore verified 93 files without writing to the ledger or refitting models.
The smoke run then completed with 15 candidates and no failures.

The first full attempt completed the candidate/fold and validation work but
failed while logging Stack metadata because an OOF reference variable was
undefined. That failed parent and its completed child runs remain in the
ledger. The defect was fixed and covered by a regression test, then the same
frozen full design was run again successfully. This repeated full attempt
exceeded the predeclared compute budget; no candidates or hyperparameters were
added or changed. The summary model table uses children of the successful
full parent, while the audit export preserves the failed attempt and smoke
records.

No test samples were used to select, tune, fit, or score T17b candidates. The
T13 frozen recipe, model, and saved test artifacts were not modified.
